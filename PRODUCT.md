# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Event guests, usually groups of two or more people, standing in front of a touchscreen at company events, trade fairs and parties run by the IT-Lab (Darmstadt). They have no instructions, a few seconds of attention and often a drink in one hand. A second audience is the team that sets the box up and runs it from the admin dashboard, often from a phone during setup.

## Product Purpose

A photo booth that cuts people out of the live camera image with a local AI model, places them in a chosen background, optionally adds playful AI effects (props that follow the face) and prints the result immediately. No data is shared with guests: no QR codes, no galleries, nothing is stored for outsiders, and all AI runs on the local server. Success: a group walks up, understands what to do within a few seconds, and leaves laughing with a print.

## Brand Personality

Playful, modern, calm. The IT-Lab brand is navy with a teal accent: confident and technical, with humor reserved for the moments where the AI does something to the photo. The interface stays quiet so the guests and their photo are the stars.

## Anti-references

Cluttered (many buttons and text at once) and childish (cute overload, emoji everywhere, cartoon styling) as it must also work in front of business customers. Not a dark UI: the rooms are often bright. Not a sterile form either.

## Design Principles

1. One obvious next step. At any moment the guest sees what to touch next; everything else recedes.
2. Show, don't explain. Live camera, real previews and the actual effect replace instructions.
3. Built for groups and for touch. Large targets, nothing at the screen edge that needs precision, readable from a step back.
4. Humor lives in the effects, not in the chrome. Controls stay calm and consistent; delight appears when the AI transforms the photo.
5. The IT-Lab identity is fixed. The logo and the navy/teal palette stay; events can add a second logo and their own start text.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Text contrast of at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text), touch targets of at least 64 px, no information conveyed by color alone, and reduced-motion alternatives for every animation. German only.
